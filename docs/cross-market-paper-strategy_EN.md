# Cross-Market Sector Rotation and Gold Paper Strategy

This document defines the execution contract for `cross_market_global_sector_rotation_v1.3_aggressive`. V1.3 covers semiconductors, storage, equipment, materials, optical modules and fiber, artificial intelligence, compute services, gaming, pharmaceuticals, MLCC, CCL/PCB, independent CPO, gold, and sector-wide range trading. Industrial metals remain out of scope for automatic entries in this phase.

ASCII abbreviations in theme mapping require alphanumeric token boundaries, so the inner `cro` in `MicroLED` cannot be mistaken for pharmaceutical `CRO`. `MicroLED`, `MiniLED`, and microcontrollers map explicitly to semiconductors, while `AIDC` maps to compute services. Chinese board names retain substring matching.

An EastMoney board-change leader must also pass an independent check against the stock's live board memberships. A source-theme mismatch, an empty live-board result, or a lookup failure fails closed in both theme prefiltering and the final per-stock decision. The check reuses the board snapshot already fetched by the order path and adds no extra network request.

V1.1 and V1.2 campaign accounts are never upgraded or mixed into V1.3 during a process restart. When new code reads a legacy strategy ID, it fails closed with `legacy_cross_market_strategy_requires_explicit_migration` before screening, selling, or submitting an order. V1.3 starts only through `POST /api/v1/vnpy-paper/cross-market-strategy/migrate`, which switches to a clean V1.3 account and restarts the campaign at `0/30`. Migration fails closed while the source has an active plan or strategy position, or when the target account already has trades; no legacy ledger or progress is overwritten.

## Signal Timing

TOPIX and Japanese equities from Yahoo Japan expose only an intraday update time, so they must also pass the Tokyo exchange's session-day calendar check. A Japanese holiday rejects the request before collection and cannot make the prior session's close time appear fresh.

- Nasdaq futures: use EastMoney code `NQ00Y` (front-month continuous E-mini Nasdaq-100) as a direction proxy only; the strategy never trades the futures contract. The realtime route maps to Sina `hf_NQ`, with Yahoo `NQ=F` as the final fallback. The existing US-technology background task samples it every 60 seconds from `09:25` through `15:00` on A-share sessions. Provider time older than 120 seconds, future timestamps, and invalid prices fail closed. Trend confirmation requires at least three distinct provider timestamps spanning 120 seconds and reports the latest 15-minute return.
  The status endpoint exposes the latest price, session move, 15-minute trend, sample count, span, thresholds, sector-score adjustment, entry permission, and sell fraction under `nasdaq_futures`.

- US close: freeze a complete theme-close universe after every US regular session. The `09:35` A-share opening decision uses only the just-completed US close theme and never treats the earlier premarket move as its opening signal. Themes cover semiconductors, storage, equipment, materials, optical networking, artificial intelligence, compute services, gaming, pharmaceuticals, MLCC, CCL, and platform benchmarks. Tickers are globally unique and each snapshot audits coverage, mean return, advancing ratio, and leader return. The storage basket uses Everpure's current NYSE ticker `P` instead of the retired `PSTG` symbol changed in April 2026. The US MLCC basket uses the still-active `TTDKY` and `MRAAY` symbols and no longer requests Kyocera's `KYOCY` ADR program, which terminated on June 5, 2026. Direct US MLCC evidence still requires both remaining symbols; when that direct premarket or close basket is unavailable, only a current same-theme Asian supply-chain signal may explicitly supplement strategy coverage. Semiconductor, storage, equipment, and materials must also pass the same-session first-hour/close composite `US_TECH_SCORE`; a missing score or a score below 40 fails closed at both theme activation and final per-stock decision, including range-entry paths.
- US premarket: freeze a separate theme universe before the US regular open, then pair it with that same session's close. The A-share `10:40`, `13:30`, and `14:30` dip scans require the prior US premarket to be strong, its same-session close not to reverse the move, and the mapped A-share board to show an intraday pullback and recovery. Premarket and close evidence can never be stitched across US sessions.
- Korea: collect KOSPI, KOSDAQ, Samsung Electronics, and SK Hynix every 60 seconds while Korea is open. Revalidate before every entry, exit, add-on, or pending-order retry. Evidence older than 120 seconds or lacking a full 300 seconds of continuous confirmation fails closed. The chain requires at least five valid samples, is anchored at the latest sample, skips duplicate or burst samples less than 45 seconds apart, and invalidates the chain when any adjacent accepted samples are more than 90 seconds apart. A normal one-minute poll therefore usually needs the sixth sample to cover the full five minutes, so rapid refreshes or five samples spanning only four minutes cannot impersonate continuous observation. A separate 60-second sell-only monitor runs while A shares are open and only when strategy-owned positions exist. Each pass revalidates Korea, the frozen A-share open, stops, take profit, trailing stop, range signals, and T+1 sellability; it performs no screening and cannot create buy orders.
- Japan and Asian supply chain: continuously collect Nikkei 225 and TOPIX before A-share decisions, plus representative MLCC names such as Murata Manufacturing, TDK, and Taiyo Yuden and CCL names such as Elite Material, Taiwan Union Technology, and ITEQ. Nikkei 225 prefers EastMoney's cash-index quote with a provider epoch; if that route fails, Sina `hf_NK` may serve as an explicitly labeled fresh Nikkei 225 futures direction proxy. TOPIX is accepted only from the Yahoo Japan cash-index page when it explicitly reports `delayMinutes=0`. Japanese equities use the same zero-delay Yahoo Japan price board, and Korean six-digit equities preserve Naver `localTradedAt`. Taiwan listed/OTC symbols use their `.TW`/`.TWO` TWSE MIS channels and derive provider trade time from the row's explicit date `d` and time `t`; the potentially shifted `tlong` is not used as freshness evidence. Taiwan Union Technology is correctly mapped as `6274.TWO`. Continuous trading still enforces the 120-second limit. The Japan lunch break may carry same-day evidence only when its provider time is near the morning halt and no more than 45 minutes old. After Japan, Korea, or Taiwan closes, only a same-day provider time from five minutes before through 60 seconds after the official close may carry until the 15:00 A-share close; it expires immediately after that boundary and never crosses a date. The Asian-theme background task covers the full A-share cash session so the `14:30` analysis can retry official-close capture. Delayed Yahoo Chart data cannot satisfy the gate. When the target US session's same-stage snapshot explicitly records insufficient OTC component coverage, strong same-theme Asian supply-chain evidence may supplement MLCC/CCL coverage and enter both active-theme selection and final per-stock entry decisions. A missing US session, stage, theme record, or merely weak US signal remains ineligible, and supplementation cannot replace the A-share pullback, board technical levels, or cost gates.
- China: freeze the CSI 300 opening gap and evaluate recovery above the stock open/VWAP together with sector strength. Each candidate also calculates 5/10/20/30/60-day moving-average support and swing support and resistance from board bars completed through the prior A-share session.
- Gold: combine the overnight COMEX gold continuous move, the 5/20-day trend through the latest New York settlement completed before the A-share pre-open, and interest-rate news published between the most recent completed A-share close and 09:15. Cuts, a pause in hikes, and an easing cycle raise the gold signal; renewed hikes, delayed cuts, and rising real yields reduce or veto it. Every gold collection statelessly fetches the Federal Reserve monetary-policy and speeches/testimony RSS feeds and requires at least one Jin10 or WallstreetCN flash-news item with a parseable publication time inside the current news window, then merges and deduplicates that live evidence with persisted intelligence context. No Intelligence source provisioning or item persistence is required. A flash-news response represents only its currently visible latest-item list and is never marked exhausted. Rate-news matches captured by an earlier live poll in the same A-share session and news window remain in the gold snapshots and are merged into the final score; cross-day carry, replay, and after-the-fact evidence remain forbidden. Failure or an empty response from either official feed, official-feed coverage that does not reach the news-window start, or no valid in-window item from either supplemental feed fails closed. Published times are normalized to timezone-aware UTC, and `news.collection` audits source success, failure, counts, window coverage, and forward-retained matches. Polls from 08:55 through 09:15 are pre-open previews only. Formal and intraday decisions after 09:15 accept a snapshot only when `news.window_end` reaches the 09:15 cutoff; a later collection failure cannot promote an earlier incomplete news window. Frozen evidence records `trend_through_date`, `quote_source`, `daily_source`, `news_window_cutoff_at`, and `news_window_complete`.

Korean realtime quotes prefer the Korea Investment & Securities KIS Open API, then fall back to Naver Finance realtime polling and Yahoo when KIS is not configured or a request fails. The Naver route covers KOSPI and KOSDAQ indices plus valid six-digit Korean equities carrying `.KS` or `.KQ`, and preserves the response `localTradedAt` as provider time; the KIS adapter uses the official intraday fields `bsop_hour`/`stck_cntg_hour`. Yahoo remains the second fallback and uses the crumb-free Chart minute endpoint when yfinance is rate-limited; its roughly 30-minute-delayed minute data cannot pass the strategy's 120-second freshness gate. US technology and CPO evidence uses a dedicated Tencent-first, Yahoo Chart fallback route so one collection pass does not share Yahoo's burst limit; this priority does not change other US analysis paths. The 91-symbol premarket theme pool subscribes through one Yahoo realtime WebSocket and preserves each symbol's millisecond trade timestamp. The 09:14 ET wake now starts and retains that stream without persisting the warm-up result as strategy evidence. Formal collection after 09:15 revalidates every stream tick against provider time; missing, older-than-120-second, at-or-after-open, or otherwise invalid symbols enter the bounded Chart HTTP gap-fill path with at most 16 workers. Close-theme collection continues to use the bounded dedicated quote route. Each HTTP request is limited to five seconds, while ordinary US quote paths retain their 15-second timeout. An incomplete premarket snapshot retries each background minute until 09:30 ET and may roll forward only same-session components whose provider trade time remains within 120 seconds and strictly before the regular open; request completion time never substitutes for trade time. Even when no theme reaches minimum coverage in one minute, the components already obtained are retained as a separate capture attempt and may fill the next minute only within those same freshness boundaries. A capture attempt cannot become a qualified theme snapshot or satisfy a trading gate. It may prove that the daily collector actually audited the configured 91-symbol universe only when it records all eleven mapped themes, at least one provider-time-validated component, and a matching close capture for the same US session; every uncovered theme still fails closed independently and never becomes a signal through the daily audit. Snapshots separately audit raw streamed quotes, fresh accepted stream components, rejected stream components, HTTP gap-fill requests, worker count, duration, reused component count, and per-symbol errors. On Windows workspaces containing non-ASCII characters, the trusted CA bundle is copied to an ASCII path under `%PROGRAMDATA%`; TLS verification remains enabled. Future, incomplete, or older-than-120-second evidence is unavailable. Configure `KIS_APP_KEY` and `KIS_APP_SECRET` in `.env`, with optional `KIS_BASE_URL` and `KIS_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` overrides. Credentials are never persisted in strategy state, logs, or acceptance artifacts.

When the four Korean quotes or the concurrent MLCC/CCL Asian-theme basket are assembled, the realtime observation timestamp is the basket's network-completion time, with collection start and duration retained for audit. During continuous trading, every provider timestamp still undergoes the future-time and 120-second freshness checks; lunch-break and official-close carry revalidate the exchange session, provider time, and A-share close boundary component by component. Per-stock entry and held-position exit revalidation both use live collection semantics instead of passing an earlier candidate or sell-quote time into network collectors. They evaluate the Korea or combined Asian gate after those requests finish and carry that completion clock into later risk signals. This prevents later Naver responses from being mislabeled as future evidence merely because they arrived a few seconds after the request batch started, without allowing an arbitrary stale value to impersonate halted-session evidence.

The five US technology quotes and three CPO quotes use the same basket-completion clock. Live scheduling determines the first-hour or close capture window at request start, then validates every provider timestamp for future time and 120-second freshness after all sequential quote requests return. These snapshots also retain collection start and duration. Replay and deterministic tests that pass an explicit `now` keep fixed point-in-time semantics. After the close, the five-symbol technology snapshot and the 91-symbol full-theme snapshot track completion independently. If either stage fails or theme coverage remains incomplete, the successful stage is not recollected while the missing stage keeps retrying during the exchange's three-minute post-close window. Available themes are unioned across same-session retries. A zero-theme pass is retained only as a separate capture attempt, and its components may fill a later pass only while their provider timestamps remain within 120 seconds; the attempt itself is never qualified close evidence. `us_close_theme_evidence_unavailable` is therefore a retryable evidence warning rather than a task crash, while unexpected collection errors still fail. Readers prefer the newest valid snapshot for each theme so a transient later failure cannot overwrite earlier qualified close evidence.

Gold and CSI 300 opening collection likewise separate the scheduling-window clock from the network-completion clock. Live scheduling verifies the gold pre-open or A-share opening window and trading day when the request starts, then validates provider future-time and 120-second freshness boundaries against completion of the quote request. The snapshot uses completion time as `observed_at` and retains `collection_started_at` and `collection_duration_seconds`. Deterministic tests that pass an explicit `now` keep fixed point-in-time semantics, so this change neither admits a new request after the window closes nor rejects a newly returned second-level quote merely because it is a few seconds later than request start.

Full-theme concurrency is enabled only for the allowlisted `YfinanceFetcher.get_cross_market_us_premarket_quotes`, `get_cross_market_us_premarket_quote`, and `get_cross_market_us_realtime_quote` methods. The batch premarket method requires `yfinance>=1.5.1`, keeps one Yahoo WebSocket alive between minute scheduler polls, and caches only trades actually received during that interval; it closes automatically after 90 seconds without a premarket collection call. Every cached quote retains the provider's original trade time, so the strategy layer still rejects values older than 120 seconds or at/after the regular-session boundary. This covers trades occurring between polls without inventing freshness from local cache time. The two dedicated Chart HTTP routes do not read or mutate shared fetcher state. The data manager retains its per-instance serialization lock for ordinary quote methods and every other provider, preserving the existing thread-safety contract.
The `korea.quote_route` block from `GET /api/v1/vnpy-paper/cross-market-strategy/status` reports the KIS, Naver, and Yahoo priority/fallback order and availability without exposing credentials or endpoint values.

The CSI 300 opening signal uses the dedicated index route and falls through available providers such as TickFlow and EastMoney when a row lacks provider time. It never substitutes local fetch time or treats a same-code stock quote as index evidence. Collection begins at the official `09:30` open, and provider time must be no earlier than that session boundary; `09:25-09:29` call-auction data cannot be frozen as the day's opening-gap evidence. Frozen snapshots expose the selected provider and session boundary through `quote_source` and `session_open_at`.

Every candidate entry, add-on, formal or intraday exit, range-exit VWAP calculation, and pending buy or sell revalidation uses the strict provider-timestamped realtime route. A regular Tushare snapshot without provider time cannot mask a later Tencent or equivalent quote carrying actual market time. Freshness is measured against completion of that network request, so a slow request cannot mislabel its newly returned second-level quote as future evidence; an unavailable route, a timestamp more than one second after completion, or evidence older than 120 seconds still fails closed.

Candidate decisions read realtime board membership, complete the per-stock Korea and Japan refresh, and only then request the candidate's strict quote. The 60-second background task continuously collects the MLCC/CCL Asian supply chain. Per-stock checks reuse a currently qualified continuous, lunch-break, or same-session close snapshot and collect the full supply-chain group inline only when that snapshot is missing or unavailable. Reuse does not relax component timestamps, coverage, or stage rules, and the final read still fails closed. Quote freshness is validated against completion of the final candidate request, so the executable A-share price remains newer than the Korea/Japan refresh. Decision start, quote-validation time, and elapsed collection time are retained for audit. A genuine second-level quote returned a few seconds after decision start is not mislabeled as future evidence; timestamps more than one second after request completion or older than 120 seconds still fail closed.

Execution priority is account drawdown fuse, hard stop, trailing stop, per-stock next-day high-open pullback exit, Korea-linked exit, `NQ00Y` technology-risk reduction, take profit, range action, then ordinary entry.

## Trading Rules

- Overlapping material and equipment terms use the narrower theme first: photoresist maps to semiconductor materials, while lithography machines and equipment map to semiconductor equipment. Only explicit semiconductor-wafer terms enter the materials theme; generic photovoltaic silicon-material/wafer boards do not.
- `NQ00Y` affects semiconductors, memory, equipment, materials, CPO/optical networking, artificial intelligence, compute services, gaming, MLCC, and CCL; it does not gate pharmaceuticals or gold. A confirmed session move at or below `-1.0%`, or a 15-minute move at or below `-0.5%`, blocks new technology entries. A session move at or below `-1.5%`, or a 15-minute move at or below `-0.8%`, sells 50% of the sellable strategy position. Outside those hard-down thresholds, futures direction adjusts the local sector score by at most `+/-5` points and can never replace US close/premarket, A-share sector, technical-level, cost, or risk evidence. CPO still bypasses the Korea/Japan direction gate but not confirmed US technology-futures risk.

- Candidate intake uses one A-share theme dictionary for the complete set of mapped sub-sectors and aliases: semiconductor design, manufacturing, packaging, analog/MCU, power devices, storage chips/modules/SSD, wafer/deposition/implant/lithography-support/metrology/packaging equipment, electronic gases/targets/CMP/package materials, CPO/800G/1.6T/optical chips/networks, AI, compute services, gaming, innovative drugs/CDMO/ADC/GLP-1/API/generics/cell and gene therapy, MLCC/passives/ceramic powder, and CCL/HDI/PCB/high-speed substrates/PCB equipment. EastMoney board-movement leaders are mapped through this dictionary, then realtime membership, board return, quote, position, liquidity, and expected edge are revalidated. The intake source theme is locked as the final trading theme and persisted with fills; unrelated bank or gold membership on the same stock cannot silently reclassify it, while realtime board evidence is still checked against that source theme. "Full mapping" means every relevant A-share sub-sector for the configured external watch universe; unrelated industries are not forced into a technology signal. Current-session turnover must be at least CNY 100 million or the candidate is rejected.
- Theme screening may still read up to 50 board leaders, while realtime per-stock gates use a bounded decision pool: four reserve candidates follow the two target slots, so the current strategy inspects at most six. Active themes are ordered by descending cross-market theme score, then sampled one candidate per theme per round; a second candidate from the highest-scoring theme is considered only after every active theme with candidates receives a first-round opportunity. A single active theme may still fill the pool on its own. A quote, technical, cost, provenance, or other hard-gate rejection is audited as a skip without consuming a buy slot; inspection stops immediately after two plans or submissions. This prevents compute or AI candidates from crowding independent active CPO, CCL, or gaming themes out of the decision pool without serially collecting realtime evidence for all 50 stocks at every checkpoint.
- Because the current account has less than two years of trading eligibility, this strategy permits new entries only on the Shanghai and Shenzhen main boards: Shenzhen prefixes `000/001/002/003` and Shanghai prefixes `600/601/603/605`. ChiNext `300/301`, STAR Market `688/689`, and Beijing Stock Exchange symbols are removed during candidate prefiltering and fail closed again before the final per-stock decision with `trading_permission_main_board_only`. The restriction applies only to new buys; any existing non-main-board strategy position can still be sold or cancelled under T+1 and risk-exit rules.
- `09:35` uses the just-completed US close as its external theme signal, then confirms Korea, Japan, the A-share open, and board technical levels. The `10:40`, `13:30`, and `14:30` scans are the premarket-led intraday dip path: prior US premarket strength, a non-reversing same-session close, an A-share pullback from the intraday high, recovery from the low, and a reclaim of the stock open or VWAP must all agree.
- Except for CPO, technology, pharmaceuticals, MLCC, and CCL pass the combined Korea/Japan market gate. Korea-linked themes additionally require the continuous five-minute Samsung Electronics, SK Hynix, and Korean-index evidence. MLCC and CCL also consume their Asian supply-chain groups. CPO bypasses only the Asian direction gate, not US timing, A-share setup, board levels, cost, or risk gates.
- The strategy reruns mapped board and stock analysis at `09:35`, `10:40`, `13:30`, and `14:30` Shanghai time. The `09:35` pass remains the only formal `vnpy_paper_auto` run that can advance the 30-day objective. The other three use independently audited intraday triggers, scope slot deduplication to the current paper account and `vnpy_paper` mode, and never count as another qualified day. Once any formal, intraday-entry, or sell-only run records a plan or submission for the session, all remaining entry slots and the five-minute formal recovery stop placing further orders; sell monitoring, active-order revalidation, and audit continue. All four share daily-order, two-position, active-order, and account-risk limits. When the frozen CSI 300 open is a high open or an extreme low open, orchestration completes position sell checks first, then records the run as `completed` and skips AlphaSift plus per-stock evidence collection. The run retains its full observation and sell-order audit, and CPO cannot bypass this market-wide gate. Persisted intraday task events retain `analysis_slot`, `formal_recovery`, `trigger_source`, and `execution_mode`, distinguishing a formal recovery, an independent intraday scan, and a slot rejected by a hard gate.
- The formal `09:35` pass must start within `[09:35, 09:37)` Shanghai time. Diagnostics persist its actual start separately, so a run record created after preparation is not misclassified as late. Once that window is missed, the ordinary formal entry fails closed and schedules the next trading day instead of creating a same-day baseline at an arbitrary intraday time.
- Board technical evidence uses only bars completed through the prior A-share session. It calculates 5/10/20/30/60-day moving averages, swing-low support, and swing-high resistance. Valid support must be at or below the current board level; a level still below support is no longer accepted merely because its absolute distance is small. Moving-average support additionally requires a non-negative slope for that period. Longer periods retain higher support weights and multi-period confluence receives a bounded bonus. A relevant board near resistance remains chase-blocked until it clears the 60-day range high by at least 1%, has a realtime board volume ratio of at least 1.5, and records two consecutive completed five-minute closes above the breakout line. Missing volume or five-minute evidence fails closed. Agent run details retain all five levels, slopes, invalidated support, breakout evidence, nearest resistance, distance, and history cutoff.
- Liquor and banks are defensive rotation references only. Their proximity to resistance adds a bounded five points only when the target board has independently confirmed support. Rotation narrative alone can never trigger an entry.
- Korea strength does not trigger a linked exit. Moderate weakness sells 50% of sellable shares; severe weakness sells all sellable shares.
- CPO bypasses the Korea/Japan direction gate and uses its own sector, leader, volume, and VWAP evidence, while retaining the phase-appropriate US close/premarket evidence, A-share opening, board levels, T+1, risk, and cost rules. Active-theme intake and final per-stock decisions share the same numeric US close, premarket, and MLCC/CCL Asian supply-chain thresholds instead of trusting a cached `strong` flag alone. CPO cannot reserve one of the two candidate slots without phase-appropriate US evidence; its independent signal may only improve the rank of CPO after that evidence passes. A core stock must carry all three internal provenance fields: an EastMoney board-movement leader source, an explicit core-stock marker, and a CPO source theme. Global rank or aggregate score cannot substitute for that provenance.
- CPO independently collects `COHR`, `LITE`, `CIEN`, and optical-networking news. Missing components are reweighted across the remaining evidence, but a complete outage of required US theme evidence fails closed. An available external score adjusts sector strength with a bounded 10% weight and cannot replace leader confirmation, volume ratio of at least 1.0, open/VWAP, board levels, cost, or risk controls.
- A CSI 300 gap of at least `+0.19%` blocks entries. A smaller positive gap does not trigger this market-wide gate and continues through the flat-open/range and other evidence checks. The raw open, prior close, and gap remain frozen for the session, while every signal read reclassifies that raw gap with the current strategy threshold. A threshold changed intraday therefore applies to the same day's evidence instead of retaining the snapshot's older classification. Status exposes the current thresholds, recorded classification, and whether reclassification changed it. The index gap alone does not liquidate a holding. The next-day high-open exit is strictly limited to the first real A-share session after each entry tranche, skipping weekends and exchange holidays. A position built on several dates exposes only the quantity eligible on the current session, and a missed first session cannot reuse this rule on an arbitrary later high open. An eligible tranche still requires the holding's own opening gap to be at least `+1.0%` and a subsequent pullback of at least `0.8%` from its session high. A stock below the opening-gap threshold continues through Korea, `NQ00Y`, take-profit, and range rules; an eligible stock that has not pulled back waits rather than selling early. A CSI 300 gap from `-1.5%` to `-0.3%` permits staged entries only after price reclaims the open/VWAP and matching realtime sectors strengthen. Sector evidence uses point-in-time EastMoney membership-board changes, aggregates only boards matching the strategy theme, uses a smooth nonlinear score to avoid saturating on a small rise, and requires at least 40. The AlphaSift stock score never substitutes for sector strength, and missing board-change evidence fails closed. A gap below `-1.5%` blocks entries. Flat opens prefer range trading, but an ordinary cross-market theme may establish an initial 25% position only after reclaiming the open/VWAP with sector score at least 60, valid support score at least 85, and complete US/Asian evidence. It may add to the 50% symbol target only at a later `10:40`, `13:30`, or `14:30` dip checkpoint with paired premarket/close and A-share pullback-rebound confirmation. Legacy positions without the audited initial flat-open reason cannot use this add-on path.
- The top-level `cn_open` status exposes the evidence `session_date` alongside the current thresholds, recorded classification, and reclassification difference for four-slot and pure-forward session auditing.
- Aggregate constituent turnover divided by aggregate constituent share volume is not a CSI 300 index-point VWAP, so the status reports `index_point_vwap_unavailable` and never accepts that ratio as reclaim evidence. Low-open candidates still use each stock's own turnover-derived VWAP; when it is unavailable, only an actual reclaim of that stock's opening price can confirm entry.
- Range mode requires `ADX(14)<20`, a near-flat 20-day moving-average slope, and an intact range floor, then uses Bollinger, RSI, and VWAP signals in at most two tranches. Indicator input excludes the unfinished current daily bar, preserves every prior completed bar, and appends the current session's live high, low, and price instead of overwriting the prior close. A fresh range-low signal may add the second tranche; no third tranche is allowed. A range-high signal removes one complete active tranche, selling all when one tranche exists or one half when two exist, while remaining capped by A-share T+1 sellability. A range-low entry is still a new buy and must pass its applicable `US_TECH_SCORE`, `NQ00Y`, and Korea/Japan composite gates; CPO bypasses only the Korea/Japan gate, while range-high exits are never blocked by entry gates.

## Execution And Costs

Replay fills use the VWAP of the first one-minute bar after the signal. Participation is capped at 5% of minute volume. Spread, one-minute volatility, participation, and dynamic `2-50bp` slippage are included. Live strict matching reads best bid and ask from the normalized Tencent or Sina quote and derives the observed spread; a missing book falls back conservatively to `4bp`, while a genuine zero spread is preserved and remains subject to the `2bp` minimum slippage. Suspensions, one-price limits, untouched limits, gaps, and zero volume can produce no or partial fills. Splits and cash dividends are supported.

A-share order quantities are validated by board. Regular Shanghai and Shenzhen shares use 100-share buy and partial-sell units, with a residual below 100 sellable only in one complete disposal. STAR Market buys require at least 200 shares and then allow one-share increments; a remaining balance below 200 must be sold in full. Beijing Stock Exchange buys require at least 100 shares and then allow one-share increments; a remaining balance below 100 must be sold in full, and vn.py orders use the `BSE` exchange. Once a declaration is valid, exchange matching may produce integer-share partial fills; the gateway retains the original declared quantity so a non-round-hundred STAR or BSE remainder can continue through later minute matches. A candidate fails closed when the current tranche budget or remaining capacity under the 50% single-stock cap cannot fund the minimum valid order.

Orders may be cancelled before execution. Default price limits are inferred as 10% for main boards, 20% for ChiNext/STAR, and 30% for Beijing-listed securities; historical frames may explicitly supply special limits such as 5%. Cross-market exits only manage net lots tagged `source=cross_market_auto_entry`, less exits tagged `source=cross_market_auto_exit`. Manual holdings are not claimed. Splits adjust each attributed lot's quantity and unit cost, while A-share T+1 eligibility is evaluated per fill lot so same-day entries and add-ons cannot be sold that day. Cross-market and ordinary automatic entries share daily order and budget limits.

Whenever the account has strategy-attributed A-share positions, formal runs, the 60-second sell-only monitor, and pending-plan revalidation first synchronize implemented corporate actions from the preceding 45 days. Configured providers, including Tushare, are tried first, followed by the AkShare EastMoney, CNInfo, and Beijing Stock Exchange announcement fallbacks. Cash dividends and splits are deduplicated by effective date, type, and value, then posted before strategy lots, costs, and sellable quantities are rebuilt. Completed symbols use a persisted daily cache and provider failures retry after 15 minutes. If every source fails, no new order is created and no exit is inferred from potentially incorrect holdings.

Pending approval and automatic retry sell plans never submit from stale reasons alone. The service reloads the account, strategy-attributed position, and a quote no older than 120 seconds, then recomputes the full priority chain: account drawdown fuse, hard stop, trailing stop, high-open exit, Korea weakness, take profit, and range exit. The current sell fraction may reduce the original plan quantity but cannot enlarge it, the submission uses the revalidated quote, and strategy ownership plus A-share T+1 are checked again. An expired trigger, stale quote, or unavailable position evidence fails closed. Neither a planned nor a submitted-but-unfilled exit clears its trailing-stop peak; cleanup occurs only after the strategy-attributed position is actually flat.

Every active, non-terminal `vnpy_paper` cross-market order is reconciled with the gateway and revalidated every 60 seconds. Manual approval, failed-plan retry, and continued waiting by an already submitted buy all rerun the current account-drawdown, consecutive-loss cooldown, and cash-floor gates. A blocked gate reduces allowed buy quantity to zero and sends any active remainder into cancellation, while sell plans remain open to reduce risk. An expired signal also cancels the unfilled remainder. Buy capacity is recomputed from current total equity, the remaining percentage capacity, the current approval price or original active limit, and fees; if the resulting quantity is below the outstanding order quantity, the original order is cancelled rather than allowed to wait at excess size. A partially filled buy continues to count its booked exposure while the symbol is excluded from the position-slot count only for revalidating that original order. Multiple fills carrying the same vn.py order ID count as one strategy tranche. Cancellation audit records the trigger, allowed and remaining quantities, and complete revalidation evidence.

A formal automatic round completes all strategy-position sell checks before evaluating account-level buy risk. When account drawdown, the consecutive-loss cooldown, or the cash floor already blocks buying, every valid non-blacklisted candidate records that account-risk reason and skips per-candidate live cross-market analysis. No reduction or exit order is blocked by this buy-only gate.

Default costs use this paper account's actual 0.008% commission on both buys and sells, with a CNY 5 minimum per order, 0.05% stock sell stamp duty, and 0.001% stock transfer fee on both sides. Exchange handling and regulatory levies are treated as included in the stated commission and are not charged twice. ETFs do not pay stock stamp duty or stock transfer fees. Instrument class comes from explicit `instrument_type/security_type` evidence or the A-share ETF code; the gold theme is not treated wholesale as an ETF, so gold-mining equities retain stock taxes. The net-edge gate estimates the actual order notional from current total equity and the target percentage returned by the current decision, then includes minimum commissions, taxes, and estimated two-sided slippage instead of assuming the initial CNY 50,000 order size. Flat-open add-on eligibility likewise measures strategy cost basis against current total equity, so account gains or losses cannot distort the original 25% tranche. Each automatic buy solves the largest order notional for which order notional plus buy commission and transfer fee stays within the target cash allocation. This prevents two simultaneous 50% targets from making the second fill unaffordable after costs. Dynamic slippage remains embedded in the fill price, while the buy limit caps fill notional at the submitted-price amount. The candidate audit stores this calculation under `cross_market_execution_budget`. All performance is net of costs and slippage. `GET /api/v1/vnpy-paper/performance` exposes cumulative fees, taxes, total transaction cost, net realized PnL, and profit factor under `trade_metrics`, and states that dynamic slippage is already embedded in fill prices.

When `created_from` is omitted, the performance endpoint uses the current paper account creation time as the lower bound for Agent-run statistics, preventing strategies executed before the account reset from entering this 30-session campaign. An explicit time window still takes precedence, and `run_window.source` identifies the selected scope. `agent_calibration_shadow` runs, which only support long-horizon comparison and cannot operate the paper account, are also excluded from performance attribution. Account equity, fills, and cost metrics are always isolated by the current account ID.

Order-capable Agent runs bind the current paper account before their audit record is created. Background retry, active-order recovery, and manual approval or retry validate both the run's account ID and the current account creation boundary. A plan created before an account reset or switch cannot be submitted into the new account even when its old `plan_uid` is known.

The local `paper` ledger records immediate fills and cannot prove a next-minute fill. Automatic orders for this strategy therefore fail closed with `cross_market_next_minute_execution_unavailable`. Use `dry_run` for plans, `vnpy_paper` for gateway execution, and the minute execution model for historical replay. The built-in `DSA_SIM` may execute this strategy only with `DSA_SIM_MATCHING_MODE=next_minute_vwap`: it freezes a strict realtime quote at signal time, reads another provider-timestamped cumulative-volume and cumulative-turnover snapshot in the next minute, derives minute VWAP and volume from the deltas, and applies the same dynamic-slippage, limit-price, and 5% participation model. The two provider timestamps must be 45–90 seconds apart; a shorter span does not prove a complete next minute, while a longer span may cross multiple bars or a sleep interval, so both cancel fail closed. Tencent's provider-supplied daily limit-up and limit-down prices are passed directly to matching so special limits such as ST 5% are preserved; an A-share quote with neither provider limits nor a previous close is rejected. A snapshot older than 120 seconds, a non-advancing timestamp, regressing cumulative values, a provider change, or inconsistent volume/turnover deltas cancels the order instead of guessing a fill. Remainders after partial fills keep waiting and remain subject to the 60-second strategy revalidation. Cross-market orders also fail closed under every other `DSA_SIM` matching mode. The read-only status response exposes mode, delay, provider-span gate, and pending baseline count under `diagnostics.vnpy_bridge.matching`.

Order-status and trade callbacks for the same vn.py order are serialized within the process. Once a trade callback commits the Portfolio fill plus `fill_sync` and `execution` audit, a later order callback or MainEngine reconciliation may only observe the completed result; it cannot overwrite next-minute VWAP, slippage, fee, or fill-link evidence with an older submitted state. The campaign report continues to fail closed when any fill lacks execution evidence.

The `DSA_SIM` opening balance is sourced from the current paper account's `initial_cash`; it cannot silently retain the gateway's own CNY 1,000,000 default. On connect and reconnect, only settings declared by the simulated gateway are retained. If an external broker settings file is accidentally referenced, account credentials and unrelated fields are discarded before the local simulator is called, keeping gateway diagnostics aligned with the CNY 100,000 campaign ledger.

## Risk And Acceptance

Every formal session that advances the 30-day target must contain fresh, continuously confirmed `NQ00Y` evidence under `nasdaq_futures_continuous_trend_available`. Missing, stale, or less-than-120-second trend evidence fails closed and may receive an intraday five-minute recheck; evidence collected after the formal run cannot retroactively qualify it.

The acceptance payload limits `qualified_execution_modes` and `qualified_trigger_sources` to the `vnpy_paper` / `vnpy_paper_auto` pair that can advance the 30-day target, and the run status must be `completed`. It discloses `dry_run` and zero-order sources that may form daily observations separately through `observation_execution_modes` and `observation_trigger_sources`; legacy `partial` run states fail closed and do not enter daily counting.

The optional point-in-time replay API accepts the same 12 tradeable V1.3 themes and explicit opening/intraday phase, US close and premarket, `NQ00Y`, Asian supply-chain and market gates, board technicals, and next-day high-open exit evidence. It is diagnostic only and cannot advance the pure-forward 30-session campaign.

US premarket and close coverage normally requires a qualified provider timestamp for each theme basket. Because the core `mlcc` and `ccl` companies are primarily listed in Japan, Korea, and Taiwan, a missing qualified US ADR or mapped-basket quote may be supplemented only by a current, strong same-theme Asian supply-chain signal. Supplementation also requires the target US session's same-stage snapshot and an explicit component-coverage-insufficient record for that theme. A missing US session or calendar, an older schema without the theme record, a stage mismatch, a weak directional signal, or any other non-coverage failure cannot be hidden by Asian evidence. The runtime status exposes evidence completeness under `supplemented_themes`; active-theme diagnostics separately expose whether the Asian close or premarket supplement passed and which score source was used. No other theme can use this route, and missing, stale, or weak Asian evidence still fails closed.

Asian supply-chain reads revalidate every component provider timestamp. If a newer collection has insufficient coverage because of a transient provider failure, the service may select the latest complete snapshot that is still valid as live, lunch-break, or same-session official-close evidence. It exposes `latest_observed_at`, `skipped_newer_snapshot_count`, and `selection_reason`; the fallback expires immediately at its validity boundary and never carries into another trading day.

Same-session formal deduplication runs at the final `run_auto_trade_once` entry point, so manual UI triggers, API calls, daily scheduling, and `cross_market_formal_recovery` share one guard. Ordinary repeated formal runs always skip; only an explicit recovery pass may recheck. A formal run that lacked evidence may recover after current evidence becomes ready when planned and submitted counts are both zero. If the 09:35 pass skipped candidates only because the CSI 300 opening snapshot had not yet been persisted, snapshot arrival is handled by this same bounded recovery path. A fully evidenced run may continue receiving five-minute rechecks only when every candidate was skipped for a transient condition: an unconfirmed low/flat-open reclaim, temporarily invalid or under-scored flat-open support, temporarily weak sector strength, a breakout still awaiting volume and consecutive five-minute confirmation, or a temporarily missing provider timestamp. Any plan or submission stops recovery immediately. High opens, extreme low opens, account-risk blocks, and other hard gates never trigger a recheck. Unavailable run or decision audit fails closed.

Every intraday formal recovery must link to an opening formal baseline created within `[09:35, 09:37)` that day and persist both `formal_recovery=true` and `intraday_entry_recheck=true` in Agent diagnostics. A recovery without that baseline, or a late unmarked `vnpy_paper_auto` run, cannot become a formal execution day or advance the 30-day count.

The default account starts at CNY 100,000, holds at most two stocks, targets 50% per stock, and caps total exposure at 100%. Every new tranche uses current total equity times the signal percentage and remains bounded by the remaining target gap; initial capital is only the campaign starting point, so fixed CNY 50,000 order/symbol limits or a CNY 100,000 daily budget cannot suppress compounding after gains. A qualifying flat-open entry starts at no more than 25% and adds only after the later intraday confirmation. A gold signal confirmed only by price is capped at 25% for the symbol, while price plus rate-news confirmation may use the full 50% slot. At most four buy orders may be submitted per day so both stocks can be built in two steps, while the two-position, 50%-per-symbol, 100%-total, and duplicate-order limits remain hard caps. Fees, taxes, dynamic slippage, A-share lot rules, and T+1 remain enforced, so full allocation is a risk target rather than a guarantee of exactly zero residual cash. Hard stop, take profit, and trailing drawdown remain 5%, 8%, and 4%. Generic maximum-holding-period and no-progress exits are forcibly disabled for V1.3 so saved settings cannot introduce an undefined sell condition. Daily-loss, account-drawdown, loss-streak, cost-edge, and T+1 protections remain unchanged.

Expected gross edge first uses an explicit candidate forecast. A range entry uses the distance from the current price to the Bollinger upper band; a confirmed intraday pullback uses the distance back to the session high; other entries may use the mapped board's distance to its nearest valid resistance. Each target is capped at the 8% take-profit level. `ATR20%` is retained only for volatility, slippage, and risk diagnostics and never substitutes for expected return. Missing every auditable target blocks the order instead of deriving a return from the ranking score.

Use `GET /api/v1/vnpy-paper/cross-market-strategy/status` for frozen US premarket/close themes, Korea, Japan, Asian supply-chain, A-share open, CPO, and gold evidence. Its `collection` blocks expose the latest session, per-stage counts, timestamps, coverage, and required-stage completeness. `us_premarket.latest_capture` separately exposes the newest real premarket snapshot's US session, age, raw-stream/fresh-stream/rejected-stream/HTTP-gap-fill/final-valid counts for the 91-symbol universe, available and missing lists for all 11 mapped themes, and raw-US full-coverage result. This audit block does not use Asian supply-chain supplementation or change the rule that the next A-share session consumes the preceding US session. Use `GET /api/v1/vnpy-paper/cross-market-strategy/acceptance` for forward-campaign progress; its `session_results` freezes the representative run, evidence status, missing requirements, execution mode, and candidate/plan/submission/skip counts for every observed session. Each row also associates that date's formal `vnpy_paper` run under `formal_execution`, including its own run state and candidate, plan, submission, and skip counts; aggregates separately disclose formal execution days, formal runs that were fully evidenced at execution time, evidence-only days, and dates completed by a later zero-order observation. Use `POST /api/v1/vnpy-paper/cross-market-strategy/acceptance/start` to start or reset the campaign. Use `POST /api/v1/vnpy-paper/cross-market-strategy/migrate` to move a legacy campaign to a clean V1.3 account and reset its count; `target_account_id` is optional. The current acceptance target is 30 distinct A-share trading days from the campaign start. It has no historical-replay prerequisite. The campaign performance curve dates initial equity on the campaign start date and the read-only account snapshot on the current observation date or the thirtieth fully evidenced session, so a zero-trade campaign still has dated endpoints.

Campaign start binds the current paper-account ID and opening equity. Later acceptance only counts Agent runs whose `settings.account_id` matches that bound account, so an account reset cannot mix two ledgers into one 30-day result. `GET /api/v1/vnpy-paper/cross-market-strategy/acceptance/report` returns an in-progress preview. Thirty fully evidenced sessions complete the forward window, but strategy acceptance additionally requires at least eight buy fills and five closed trades. A completed campaign below either threshold returns `final_insufficient_sample` with `strategy_accepted=false`; zero or sparse trading can never be presented as a passed strategy. The report includes net performance, drawdown, fees, taxes, the equity curve, and campaign transactions sourced from `cross_market_auto_entry` / `cross_market_auto_exit`. Every `DSA_SIM` fill carries its next-minute VWAP reference, model and realized slippage in bp, spread, minute volatility, participation, and directional slippage cost; aggregates expose explicit fees and taxes, slippage cost, all-in trading cost, and evidence coverage. `decision_audit` retains each formal `vnpy_paper` session's compact per-candidate source theme, action, status, blocker, score, data quality, risk flags, and trade plan. The primary equity curve, daily returns, and drawdown use persisted daily account snapshots, preserving zero-trade days and mark-to-market PnL, while the fill-level curve remains available as `execution_equity_curve`. A daily snapshot counts as closing evidence only when it was updated at or after `14:55` Asia/Shanghai on that A-share session; earlier snapshots are listed under `non_closing_dates`. The `cross_market_campaign_closing_snapshot` background task checks every five minutes and persists exactly one snapshot for the bound campaign account after 14:55 only when the current date is confirmed as an A-share trading day. It fails closed when the calendar or account ownership is uncertain, requires no Web-page visit, and never backfills a historical date. A fill without slippage evidence or an observation day without a closing snapshot produces an explicit warning.
