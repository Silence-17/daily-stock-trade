# Cross-Market Sector Rotation and Gold Paper Strategy

This document defines the execution contract for `cross_market_global_sector_rotation_v1.4_staged`. V1.4 covers semiconductors, storage, equipment, materials, optical modules and fiber, artificial intelligence, compute services, gaming, pharmaceuticals, consumer, banks, utilities, energy, industrials, MLCC, CCL/PCB, independent CPO, gold, and sector-wide range trading. Industrial metals remain out of scope for automatic entries in this phase.

ASCII abbreviations in theme mapping require alphanumeric token boundaries, so the inner `cro` in `MicroLED` cannot be mistaken for pharmaceutical `CRO`. `MicroLED`, `MiniLED`, and microcontrollers map explicitly to semiconductors, while `AIDC` maps to compute services. Chinese board names retain substring matching.

An EastMoney board-change leader must also pass an independent check against the stock's live board memberships. A source-theme mismatch, an empty live-board result, or a lookup failure fails closed in both theme prefiltering and the final per-stock decision. The check reuses the board snapshot already fetched by the order path and adds no extra network request.

V1.1, V1.2, and V1.3 campaign accounts are never upgraded or mixed into V1.4 during a process restart. A legacy strategy ID fails closed with `legacy_cross_market_strategy_requires_explicit_migration` before screening, selling, or ordering. `POST /api/v1/vnpy-paper/cross-market-strategy/migrate` enables V1.4 and restarts the campaign at `0/30`. A flat source may use a clean target account. A source with positions may be adopted in place only when there is no active plan and every open share is fully attributable to `cross_market_auto_entry`; uncertain ownership or quantity fails closed and no legacy ledger is overwritten.

## Signal Timing

TOPIX and Japanese equities from Yahoo Japan expose only an intraday update time, so they must also pass the Tokyo exchange's session-day calendar check. A Japanese holiday rejects the request before collection and cannot make the prior session's close time appear fresh.

- Nasdaq futures: use EastMoney code `NQ00Y` (front-month continuous E-mini Nasdaq-100) as a direction proxy only; the strategy never trades the futures contract. The realtime route maps to Sina `hf_NQ`, with Yahoo `NQ=F` as the final fallback. The existing US-technology background task samples it every 60 seconds from `09:25` through `15:00` on A-share sessions. Provider time older than 120 seconds, future timestamps, and invalid prices fail closed. Trend confirmation requires at least three distinct provider timestamps spanning 120 seconds and reports the latest 15-minute return.
  The status endpoint exposes the latest price, session move, 15-minute trend, sample count, span, thresholds, sector-score adjustment, entry permission, and sell fraction under `nasdaq_futures`.

- US close: freeze a complete theme-close universe after every US regular session. The `09:30` A-share opening decision uses only the just-completed US close theme and never treats the earlier premarket move as its opening signal. Themes cover semiconductors, storage, equipment, materials, optical networking, artificial intelligence, compute services, gaming, pharmaceuticals, MLCC, CCL, and platform benchmarks. Tickers are globally unique and each snapshot audits coverage, mean return, advancing ratio, and leader return. The storage basket uses Everpure's current NYSE ticker `P` instead of the retired `PSTG` symbol changed in April 2026. The US MLCC basket uses the still-active `TTDKY` and `MRAAY` symbols and no longer requests Kyocera's `KYOCY` ADR program, which terminated on June 5, 2026. Direct US MLCC evidence still requires both remaining symbols; when that direct premarket or close basket is unavailable, only a current same-theme Asian supply-chain signal may explicitly supplement strategy coverage. Semiconductor, storage, equipment, and materials must also pass the same-session first-hour/close composite `US_TECH_SCORE`; a missing score or a score below 40 fails closed at both theme activation and final per-stock decision, including range-entry paths.
- US premarket: freeze a separate theme universe before the US regular open, then pair it with that same session's close for evidence and replay diagnostics. The formal live A-share window remains `09:30-09:35`; `10:40` may recheck only candidates already audited as recoverable in the opening baseline and cannot expand the universe. The `13:30` and `14:30` slots rescreen independently, but new buys are allowed only when same-day whole-market breadth proves divergence, an extreme-decline setup, or repair. Premarket and close evidence can never be stitched across US sessions.
- Korea: collect KOSPI, KOSDAQ, Samsung Electronics, and SK Hynix every 60 seconds while Korea is open. Revalidate before every entry, exit, add-on, or pending-order retry. Evidence older than 120 seconds or lacking a full 300 seconds of continuous confirmation fails closed. The chain requires at least five valid samples, is anchored at the latest sample, skips duplicate or burst samples less than 45 seconds apart, and invalidates the chain when any adjacent accepted samples are more than 90 seconds apart. A normal one-minute poll therefore usually needs the sixth sample to cover the full five minutes, so rapid refreshes or five samples spanning only four minutes cannot impersonate continuous observation. A separate 60-second sell-only monitor creates an Agent run only when at least one strategy-owned position has T+1-sellable quantity. If every lot is same-day and unsellable, it skips before corporate-action sync and exit-signal requests; otherwise it revalidates Korea, the frozen A-share open, stops, take profit, trailing stop, and range signals. It performs no screening and cannot create buy orders.
- Japan and Asian supply chain: continuously collect Nikkei 225 and TOPIX before A-share decisions, plus representative MLCC names such as Murata Manufacturing, TDK, and Taiyo Yuden and CCL names such as Elite Material, Taiwan Union Technology, and ITEQ. Nikkei 225 prefers EastMoney's cash-index quote with a provider epoch; if that route fails, Sina `hf_NK` may serve as an explicitly labeled fresh Nikkei 225 futures direction proxy. TOPIX is accepted only from the Yahoo Japan cash-index page when it explicitly reports `delayMinutes=0`. Japanese equities use the same zero-delay Yahoo Japan price board, and Korean six-digit equities preserve Naver `localTradedAt`. Taiwan listed/OTC symbols use their `.TW`/`.TWO` TWSE MIS channels and derive provider trade time from the row's explicit date `d` and time `t`; the potentially shifted `tlong` is not used as freshness evidence. Taiwan Union Technology is correctly mapped as `6274.TWO`. Continuous trading still enforces the 120-second limit. Material Korea/Japan downside is a hard entry veto only for technology-weighted themes: semiconductors, memory, equipment, materials, CPO/CCL/MLCC, AI, compute services, and gaming. Pharmaceuticals treat Asian direction as scoring context instead of a veto. Qualified same-theme Asian evidence remains mandatory for MLCC/CCL and cannot replace A-share, technical, or cost gates.
- China: freeze the CSI 300 opening gap and evaluate recovery above the stock open/VWAP together with sector strength. Each candidate also calculates 5/10/20/30/60-day moving-average support and swing support and resistance from board bars completed through the prior A-share session.
- Whole-market breadth: all four entry slots collect A-share advancing, declining, and flat issue counts and persist the current sample, daily sample count, and intraday maxima/minima. A prior sample with more advancers than decliners followed by fewer advancers and more decliners is divergence. A current declining count at or above the configurable threshold (default 4,000) is an extreme-decline setup. After such a sample, more advancers together with fewer decliners is repair. Intraday risk collection prefers EastMoney's provider-timestamped, fully paginated snapshot and rejects the entire batch if any page is missing. Full-universe breadth gets a dedicated 35-second evidence budget to accommodate the provider's 30-second request ceiling; smaller index evidence retains the 15-second budget. Missing provider timestamps or incomplete timestamp coverage fails closed.
- Gold: combine the overnight COMEX gold continuous move, the 5/20-day trend through the latest New York settlement completed before the A-share pre-open, and interest-rate news published between the most recent completed A-share close and 09:15. Cuts, a pause in hikes, and an easing cycle raise the gold signal; renewed hikes, delayed cuts, and rising real yields reduce or veto it. Every gold collection statelessly fetches the Federal Reserve monetary-policy and speeches/testimony RSS feeds and requires at least one Jin10 or WallstreetCN flash-news item with a parseable publication time inside the current news window, then merges and deduplicates that live evidence with persisted intelligence context. No Intelligence source provisioning or item persistence is required. A flash-news response represents only its currently visible latest-item list and is never marked exhausted. Rate-news matches captured by an earlier live poll in the same A-share session and news window remain in the gold snapshots and are merged into the final score; cross-day carry, replay, and after-the-fact evidence remain forbidden. Failure or an empty response from either official feed, official-feed coverage that does not reach the news-window start, or no valid in-window item from either supplemental feed fails closed. Published times are normalized to timezone-aware UTC, and `news.collection` audits source success, failure, counts, window coverage, and forward-retained matches. Polls from 08:55 through 09:15 are pre-open previews only. Formal and intraday decisions after 09:15 accept a snapshot only when `news.window_end` reaches the 09:15 cutoff; a later collection failure cannot promote an earlier incomplete news window. Frozen evidence records `trend_through_date`, `quote_source`, `daily_source`, `news_window_cutoff_at`, and `news_window_complete`.

Korean realtime quotes prefer the Korea Investment & Securities KIS Open API, then fall back to Naver Finance realtime polling and Yahoo when KIS is not configured or a request fails. The Naver route covers KOSPI and KOSDAQ indices plus valid six-digit Korean equities carrying `.KS` or `.KQ`, and preserves the response `localTradedAt` as provider time; the KIS adapter uses the official intraday fields `bsop_hour`/`stck_cntg_hour`. Yahoo remains the second fallback and uses the crumb-free Chart minute endpoint when yfinance is rate-limited; its roughly 30-minute-delayed minute data cannot pass the strategy's 120-second freshness gate. US technology and CPO evidence uses a dedicated Tencent-first, Yahoo Chart fallback route so one collection pass does not share Yahoo's burst limit; this priority does not change other US analysis paths. The 91-symbol premarket theme pool subscribes through one Yahoo realtime WebSocket and preserves each symbol's millisecond trade timestamp. The 09:14 ET wake now starts and retains that stream without persisting the warm-up result as strategy evidence. Formal collection after 09:15 revalidates every stream tick against provider time; missing, older-than-120-second, at-or-after-open, or otherwise invalid symbols enter the bounded Chart HTTP gap-fill path with at most 16 workers. Close-theme collection continues to use the bounded dedicated quote route. Each HTTP request is limited to five seconds, while ordinary US quote paths retain their 15-second timeout. An incomplete premarket snapshot retries each background minute until 09:30 ET and may roll forward only same-session components whose provider trade time remains within 120 seconds and strictly before the regular open; request completion time never substitutes for trade time. Even when no theme reaches minimum coverage in one minute, the components already obtained are retained as a separate capture attempt and may fill the next minute only within those same freshness boundaries. A capture attempt cannot become a qualified theme snapshot or satisfy a trading gate. It may prove that the daily collector actually audited the configured 91-symbol universe only when it records all eleven mapped themes, at least one provider-time-validated component, and a matching close capture for the same US session; every uncovered theme still fails closed independently and never becomes a signal through the daily audit. Snapshots separately audit raw streamed quotes, fresh accepted stream components, rejected stream components, HTTP gap-fill requests, worker count, duration, reused component count, and per-symbol errors. On Windows workspaces containing non-ASCII characters, the trusted CA bundle is copied to an ASCII path under `%PROGRAMDATA%`; TLS verification remains enabled. Future, incomplete, or older-than-120-second evidence is unavailable. Configure `KIS_APP_KEY` and `KIS_APP_SECRET` in `.env`, with optional `KIS_BASE_URL` and `KIS_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` overrides. Credentials are never persisted in strategy state, logs, or acceptance artifacts.

When the four Korean quotes or the concurrent MLCC/CCL Asian-theme basket are assembled, the realtime observation timestamp is the basket's network-completion time, with collection start and duration retained for audit. During continuous trading, every provider timestamp still undergoes the future-time and 120-second freshness checks; lunch-break and official-close carry revalidate the exchange session, provider time, and A-share close boundary component by component. Per-stock entry and held-position exit revalidation both use live collection semantics instead of passing an earlier candidate or sell-quote time into network collectors. They evaluate the Korea or combined Asian gate after those requests finish and carry that completion clock into later risk signals. This prevents later Naver responses from being mislabeled as future evidence merely because they arrived a few seconds after the request batch started, without allowing an arbitrary stale value to impersonate halted-session evidence.

The five US technology quotes and three CPO quotes use the same basket-completion clock. Live scheduling determines the first-hour or close capture window at request start, then validates every provider timestamp for future time and 120-second freshness after all sequential quote requests return. These snapshots also retain collection start and duration. Replay and deterministic tests that pass an explicit `now` keep fixed point-in-time semantics. After the close, the five-symbol technology snapshot and the 91-symbol full-theme snapshot track completion independently. If either stage fails or theme coverage remains incomplete, the successful stage is not recollected while the missing stage keeps retrying during the exchange's three-minute post-close window. Available themes are unioned across same-session retries. A zero-theme pass is retained only as a separate capture attempt, and its components may fill a later pass only while their provider timestamps remain within 120 seconds; the attempt itself is never qualified close evidence. `us_close_theme_evidence_unavailable` is therefore a retryable evidence warning rather than a task crash, while unexpected collection errors still fail. Readers prefer the newest valid snapshot for each theme so a transient later failure cannot overwrite earlier qualified close evidence.

Gold and CSI 300 opening collection likewise separate the scheduling-window clock from the network-completion clock. Live scheduling verifies the gold pre-open or A-share opening window and trading day when the request starts, then validates provider future-time and 120-second freshness boundaries against completion of the quote request. The snapshot uses completion time as `observed_at` and retains `collection_started_at` and `collection_duration_seconds`. Deterministic tests that pass an explicit `now` keep fixed point-in-time semantics, so this change neither admits a new request after the window closes nor rejects a newly returned second-level quote merely because it is a few seconds later than request start.

Full-theme concurrency is enabled only for the allowlisted `YfinanceFetcher.get_cross_market_us_premarket_quotes`, `get_cross_market_us_premarket_quote`, and `get_cross_market_us_realtime_quote` methods. The batch premarket method requires `yfinance>=1.5.1`, keeps one Yahoo WebSocket alive between minute scheduler polls, and caches only trades actually received during that interval; it closes automatically after 90 seconds without a premarket collection call. Every cached quote retains the provider's original trade time, so the strategy layer still rejects values older than 120 seconds or at/after the regular-session boundary. This covers trades occurring between polls without inventing freshness from local cache time. The two dedicated Chart HTTP routes do not read or mutate shared fetcher state. The data manager retains its per-instance serialization lock for ordinary quote methods and every other provider, preserving the existing thread-safety contract.
The `korea.quote_route` block from `GET /api/v1/vnpy-paper/cross-market-strategy/status` reports the KIS, Naver, and Yahoo priority/fallback order and availability without exposing credentials or endpoint values.

The CSI 300 opening signal uses the dedicated index route and falls through available providers such as TickFlow and EastMoney when a row lacks provider time. It never substitutes local fetch time or treats a same-code stock quote as index evidence. Collection begins at the official `09:30` open, and provider time must be no earlier than that session boundary; `09:25-09:29` call-auction data cannot be frozen as the day's opening-gap evidence. Frozen snapshots expose the selected provider and session boundary through `quote_source` and `session_open_at`.

Every candidate entry, add-on, formal or intraday exit, range-exit VWAP calculation, and pending buy or sell revalidation uses the strict provider-timestamped realtime route. A regular Tushare snapshot without provider time cannot mask a later Tencent or equivalent quote carrying actual market time. Freshness is measured against completion of that network request, so a slow request cannot mislabel its newly returned second-level quote as future evidence; an unavailable route, a timestamp more than one second after completion, or evidence older than 120 seconds still fails closed.

Candidate decisions read realtime board membership, complete the per-stock Korea and Japan refresh, and only then request the candidate's strict quote. The 60-second background task continuously collects the MLCC/CCL Asian supply chain. Per-stock checks reuse a currently qualified continuous, lunch-break, or same-session close snapshot and collect the full supply-chain group inline only when that snapshot is missing or unavailable. Reuse does not relax component timestamps, coverage, or stage rules, and the final read still fails closed. Quote freshness is validated against completion of the final candidate request, so the executable A-share price remains newer than the Korea/Japan refresh. Decision start, quote-validation time, and elapsed collection time are retained for audit. A genuine second-level quote returned a few seconds after decision start is not mislabeled as future evidence; timestamps more than one second after request completion or older than 120 seconds still fail closed.

Execution priority is account drawdown fuse, hard stop, trailing stop, per-stock next-day high-open pullback exit, Korea-linked exit, `NQ00Y` technology-risk reduction, take profit, range action, then ordinary entry.

## Trading Rules

- Overlapping material and equipment terms use the narrower theme first: photoresist maps to semiconductor materials, while lithography machines and equipment map to semiconductor equipment. Only explicit semiconductor-wafer terms enter the materials theme; generic photovoltaic silicon-material/wafer boards do not.
- `NQ00Y` affects semiconductors, memory, equipment, materials, CPO/optical networking, artificial intelligence, compute services, gaming, MLCC, and CCL; it does not gate pharmaceuticals or gold. A confirmed session move at or below `-1.0%`, or a 15-minute move at or below `-0.5%`, blocks new technology entries. A session move at or below `-1.5%`, or a 15-minute move at or below `-0.8%`, sells 50% of the sellable strategy position. Outside those hard-down thresholds, futures direction adjusts the local sector score by at most `+/-5` points and can never replace US close/premarket, A-share sector, technical-level, cost, or risk evidence. CPO is technology-weighted and no longer bypasses material Korea/Japan downside.

- Candidate intake uses one A-share theme dictionary for the mapped external watch universe and the domestic-rotation pool: semiconductor design, manufacturing, packaging, analog/MCU, power devices, storage chips/modules/SSD, wafer/deposition/implant/lithography-support/metrology/packaging equipment, electronic gases/targets/CMP/package materials, CPO/800G/1.6T/optical chips/networks, AI, compute services, gaming, innovative drugs/CDMO/ADC/GLP-1/API/generics/cell and gene therapy, consumer, banks, utilities, energy, industrials, MLCC/passives/ceramic powder, and CCL/HDI/PCB/high-speed substrates/PCB equipment. EastMoney board-movement leaders are mapped through this dictionary, then realtime membership, board return, quote, position, liquidity, and expected edge are revalidated. The intake source theme is locked as the final trading theme and persisted with fills; unrelated membership cannot silently reclassify it, while realtime board evidence is still checked against that source theme. Domestic rotation boards are never forced into a technology signal. Current-session turnover must be at least CNY 100 million or the candidate is rejected.
- Theme screening may still read up to 50 board leaders, while realtime per-stock gates use a bounded pool of at most six. Technology themes must first pass their US, Nasdaq-futures, and Asian direction gates. When overseas technology risk disables them, pharmaceutical, consumer, bank, utility, energy, and industrial candidates already present in the domestic screen may enter the scoring pool as domestic-rotation fallbacks; gaming and other technology themes cannot use that fallback. Board daily history uses bounded EastMoney retries, then an independently sourced THS board with an exact name or a narrowly defined sector proxy such as bank or travel-and-hotel; the audit records `history_source=ths_fallback`, proxy name, and proxy type. Same-session memory cache remains the final fallback, and missing evidence still fails closed. A quote, technical, cost, provenance, or other hard-gate rejection is audited as a skip without consuming a buy slot; inspection stops after two plans or submissions. A qualifying high or extreme-low CSI 300 open blocks new buys throughout the entire `09:30-09:35` watch window.
- Because the current account has less than two years of trading eligibility, this strategy permits new entries only on the Shanghai and Shenzhen main boards: Shenzhen prefixes `000/001/002/003` and Shanghai prefixes `600/601/603/605`. ChiNext `300/301`, STAR Market `688/689`, and Beijing Stock Exchange symbols are removed during candidate prefiltering and fail closed again before the final per-stock decision with `trading_permission_main_board_only`. The restriction applies only to new buys; any existing non-main-board strategy position can still be sold or cancelled under T+1 and risk-exit rules.
- During `09:30-09:35`, technology-weighted themes use the just-completed US close as their external primary signal, then confirm Nasdaq futures, Korea, Japan, the A-share open, and board technical levels. Domestic rotation defaults to `10/35/25/20/10` for cross-market, A-share sector, stock price/volume, intraday confirmation, and technical evidence; overseas direction is not a hard veto. Both that profile and the technology/gold `30/25/20/15/10` profile are account-factor settings and are normalized by their totals. A domestic-rotation entry below both its open and VWAP is capped at 25% of equity unless it is a qualified range-low or strict core-leader setup. The bounded `[10:40,10:42)` recovery unions candidate audits from every same-account formal run that day, then revisits only recoverable symbols without buy activity. The `13:30` and `14:30` slots rescreen boards and stocks independently after the breadth gate passes.
- Technology-weighted themes require phase-appropriate US evidence, Nasdaq-futures confirmation, and Korea/Japan direction evidence. Korea-linked themes still require continuous five-minute Samsung Electronics, SK Hynix, and Korean-index evidence, and MLCC/CCL still require strong Asian supply-chain groups. Pharmaceuticals bypass only overseas direction vetoes, not the A-share open, local-sector strength, realtime turnover, VWAP, technical, score, cost, position, or account-risk gates.
- `cross_market_intraday_entry_scan` reruns the mapped board and stock analysis every 15 seconds during `[09:30, 09:35)` and audits `[10:40,10:42)`, `[13:30,13:32)`, and `[14:30,14:32)` once each. The first pass creates the formal `vnpy_paper_auto` baseline; 10:40 accepts only symbol-identified recoverable candidates from all same-account formal runs that day. The two late slots rescreen independently and proceed only after the whole-market breadth gate passes. Active buys, positions, cash, and account risk remain duplicate and risk inputs.
- Every candidate receives the same 100-point score. Default A/B/C thresholds remain configurable from the account factor dashboard. Grade C is valid at 10:40, 13:30, and 14:30. Every 13:30/14:30 entry, regardless of grade, uses the reduced tranche cap (25% on the current execution account) and still must pass technical, edge, cost, exposure, and risk gates. There is no mandatory daily order.
- The formal baseline window is `[09:30, 09:35)` Shanghai time. Missing the whole window prevents 10:40 from backfilling a baseline or expanding the candidate universe, but 13:30/14:30 may still persist breadth and independently rescreen when a late-entry breadth condition is present.
- Board technical evidence uses only bars completed through the prior A-share session and calculates 5/10/20/30/60-day support and resistance. If the provider fails after a successful same-session load, the service reuses that in-memory frame and audits `history_source` plus cache age; without a qualified cache it still fails closed and may be retried only through the bounded 10:40 candidate recovery. The candidate's mapped source board is the only primary technical board. If that requested board is unavailable, the whole technical signal fails closed instead of promoting a secondary style board such as “yesterday high volatility.” Related boards are secondary pressure advisories and cannot negate valid source-board support. Support proximity is a score bonus, never an entry requirement. A 5/10-day pressure reduces the technical score. A 20/30/60-day pressure reduces the score further. The sole pressure hard block requires all three conditions: primary-board 20/30/60-day pressure, a price breakout that failed volume-price confirmation, and the stock still below VWAP. Breakout confirmation still requires at least a 1% clearance, board volume ratio of at least 1.5, and two completed five-minute closes above the tested level.
- Liquor and banks remain defensive rotation references for technology themes. Their proximity to resistance adds a bounded five points only when a technology target board has independently confirmed support. A bank candidate in the domestic-rotation pool is judged on its own sector, stock, intraday, technical, cost, and risk evidence and cannot award itself that reference bonus.
- Korea strength does not trigger a linked exit. Moderate weakness sells 50% of sellable shares; severe weakness sells all sellable shares.
- CPO uses its own sector, leader, volume, and VWAP evidence while also applying the technology-theme US close/premarket, Nasdaq-futures, and material Korea/Japan downside gates, plus the A-share opening, board levels, T+1, risk, and cost rules. Active-theme intake and final per-stock decisions share the same numeric US close, premarket, and MLCC/CCL Asian supply-chain thresholds instead of trusting a cached `strong` flag alone. CPO cannot reserve one of the two candidate slots without phase-appropriate US evidence; its independent signal may only improve the rank of CPO after that evidence passes. A core stock must carry all three internal provenance fields: an EastMoney board-movement leader source, an explicit core-stock marker, and a CPO source theme. Global rank or aggregate score cannot substitute for that provenance.
- CPO independently collects `COHR`, `LITE`, `CIEN`, and optical-networking news. Missing components are reweighted across the remaining evidence, but a complete outage of required US theme evidence fails closed. An available external score adjusts sector strength with a bounded 10% weight and cannot replace leader confirmation, volume ratio of at least 1.0, open/VWAP, board levels, cost, or risk controls.
- A CSI 300 gap at or above the account-configured high-open threshold blocks chasing throughout `09:30-09:35`. At 10:40, only an opening recoverable candidate that has pulled back, rebounded, and reclaimed the open or VWAP can continue. The index gap alone never liquidates a holding, and missing critical evidence still fails closed.
- The top-level `cn_open` status exposes the evidence `session_date` alongside the current thresholds, recorded classification, and reclassification difference for four-slot and pure-forward session auditing.
- Aggregate constituent turnover divided by aggregate constituent share volume is not a CSI 300 index-point VWAP, so the status reports `index_point_vwap_unavailable` and never accepts that ratio as reclaim evidence. Low-open candidates still use each stock's own turnover-derived VWAP; when it is unavailable, only an actual reclaim of that stock's opening price can confirm entry.
- Range mode requires `ADX(14)<20`, a near-flat 20-day moving-average slope, and an intact range floor, then uses Bollinger, RSI, and VWAP signals in at most two tranches. Indicator input excludes the unfinished current daily bar, preserves every prior completed bar, and appends the current session's live high, low, and price instead of overwriting the prior close. A fresh range-low signal may add the second tranche; no third tranche is allowed. A range-high signal removes one complete active tranche, selling all when one tranche exists or one half when two exist, while remaining capped by A-share T+1 sellability. A technology range-low entry still passes its applicable `US_TECH_SCORE`, `NQ00Y`, and Korea/Japan composite gates; non-technology themes use domestic evidence and common risk controls, while range-high exits are never blocked by entry gates.

## Execution And Costs

Replay fills use the VWAP of the first one-minute bar after the signal. Participation is capped at 5% of minute volume. Spread, one-minute volatility, participation, and dynamic `2-50bp` slippage are included. Live strict matching reads best bid and ask from the normalized Tencent or Sina quote and derives the observed spread; a missing book falls back conservatively to `4bp`, while a genuine zero spread is preserved and remains subject to the `2bp` minimum slippage. Suspensions, one-price limits, untouched limits, gaps, and zero volume can produce no or partial fills. Splits and cash dividends are supported.

A-share order quantities are validated by board. Regular Shanghai and Shenzhen shares use 100-share buy and partial-sell units, with a residual below 100 sellable only in one complete disposal. STAR Market buys require at least 200 shares and then allow one-share increments; a remaining balance below 200 must be sold in full. Beijing Stock Exchange buys require at least 100 shares and then allow one-share increments; a remaining balance below 100 must be sold in full, and vn.py orders use the `BSE` exchange. Once a declaration is valid, exchange matching may produce integer-share partial fills; the gateway retains the original declared quantity so a non-round-hundred STAR or BSE remainder can continue through later minute matches. A candidate fails closed when the current tranche budget or remaining capacity under the 50% single-stock cap cannot fund the minimum valid order.

Orders may be cancelled before execution. Default price limits are inferred as 10% for main boards, 20% for ChiNext/STAR, and 30% for Beijing-listed securities; historical frames may explicitly supply special limits such as 5%. Cross-market exits only manage net lots tagged `source=cross_market_auto_entry`, less exits tagged `source=cross_market_auto_exit`. Manual holdings are not claimed. Splits adjust each attributed lot's quantity and unit cost, while A-share T+1 eligibility is evaluated per fill lot so same-day entries and add-ons cannot be sold that day. Cross-market and ordinary automatic entries share daily order and budget limits.

Whenever the account has strategy-attributed A-share positions, formal runs and pending-plan revalidation first synchronize implemented corporate actions from the preceding 45 days. The 60-second sell-only monitor performs that sync only after finding strategy-attributed T+1-sellable quantity. Configured providers, including Tushare, are tried first, followed by the AkShare EastMoney, CNInfo, and Beijing Stock Exchange announcement fallbacks. Cash dividends and splits are deduplicated by effective date, type, and value, then posted before strategy lots, costs, and sellable quantities are rebuilt. Completed symbols use a persisted daily cache and provider failures retry after 15 minutes. If every source fails, no new order is created and no exit is inferred from potentially incorrect holdings.

Pending approval and automatic retry sell plans never submit from stale reasons alone. The service reloads the account, strategy-attributed position, and a quote no older than 120 seconds, then recomputes the full priority chain: account drawdown fuse, hard stop, trailing stop, high-open exit, Korea weakness, take profit, and range exit. The current sell fraction may reduce the original plan quantity but cannot enlarge it, the submission uses the revalidated quote, and strategy ownership plus A-share T+1 are checked again. An expired trigger, stale quote, or unavailable position evidence fails closed. Neither a planned nor a submitted-but-unfilled exit clears its trailing-stop peak; cleanup occurs only after the strategy-attributed position is actually flat.

Every active, non-terminal `vnpy_paper` cross-market order is reconciled with the gateway and revalidated every 60 seconds. Manual approval, failed-plan retry, and continued waiting by an already submitted buy all rerun the current account-drawdown, consecutive-loss cooldown, and cash-floor gates. A blocked gate reduces allowed buy quantity to zero and sends any active remainder into cancellation, while sell plans remain open to reduce risk. An expired signal also cancels the unfilled remainder. Buy capacity is recomputed from current total equity, the remaining percentage capacity, the current approval price or original active limit, and fees; if the resulting quantity is below the outstanding order quantity, the original order is cancelled rather than allowed to wait at excess size. A partially filled buy continues to count its booked exposure while the symbol is excluded from the position-slot count only for revalidating that original order. Multiple fills carrying the same vn.py order ID count as one strategy tranche. Cancellation audit records the trigger, allowed and remaining quantities, and complete revalidation evidence.

A 5% hard stop uses the current daily limit-down price as a protective limit, while the actual fill still comes from the next real one-minute VWAP, observed spread, slippage, and liquidity. It therefore does not manufacture a fill at the limit-down price, and a one-price limit-down market may remain unfilled. If an older hard-stop sell stays active with `limit_not_touched` and current evidence permits a lower protective limit, the 60-second revalidation cancels it before the sell-only monitor resubmits from a fresh quote; active-order deduplication remains enforced. Ordinary take-profit, range, and trailing exits continue to use the current quote as their limit.

A formal automatic round completes all strategy-position sell checks before evaluating account-level buy risk. When account drawdown, the consecutive-loss cooldown, or the cash floor already blocks buying, every valid non-blacklisted candidate records that account-risk reason and skips per-candidate live cross-market analysis. No reduction or exit order is blocked by this buy-only gate.

Default costs use this paper account's actual 0.008% commission on both buys and sells, with a CNY 5 minimum per order, 0.05% stock sell stamp duty, and 0.001% stock transfer fee on both sides. Exchange handling and regulatory levies are treated as included in the stated commission and are not charged twice. ETFs do not pay stock stamp duty or stock transfer fees. Instrument class comes from explicit `instrument_type/security_type` evidence or the A-share ETF code; the gold theme is not treated wholesale as an ETF, so gold-mining equities retain stock taxes. The net-edge gate estimates the actual order notional from current total equity and the target percentage returned by the current decision, then includes minimum commissions, taxes, and estimated two-sided slippage instead of assuming the initial CNY 50,000 order size. Existing strategy cost basis is measured against current total equity so an add-on fills only the remaining gap to the 50% target. Each automatic buy solves the largest order notional for which order notional plus buy commission and transfer fee stays within the target cash allocation. This prevents two simultaneous 50% targets from making the second fill unaffordable after costs. Dynamic slippage remains embedded in the fill price, while the buy limit caps fill notional at the submitted-price amount. The candidate audit stores this calculation under `cross_market_execution_budget`. All performance is net of costs and slippage. `GET /api/v1/vnpy-paper/performance` exposes cumulative fees, taxes, total transaction cost, net realized PnL, and profit factor under `trade_metrics`, and states that dynamic slippage is already embedded in fill prices.

When `created_from` is omitted, the performance endpoint uses the current paper account creation time as the lower bound for Agent-run statistics, preventing strategies executed before the account reset from entering this 30-session campaign. An explicit time window still takes precedence, and `run_window.source` identifies the selected scope. `agent_calibration_shadow` runs, which only support long-horizon comparison and cannot operate the paper account, are also excluded from performance attribution. Account equity, fills, and cost metrics are always isolated by the current account ID.

Order-capable Agent runs bind the current paper account before their audit record is created. Background retry, active-order recovery, and manual approval or retry validate both the run's account ID and the current account creation boundary. A plan created before an account reset or switch cannot be submitted into the new account even when its old `plan_uid` is known.

The local `paper` ledger records immediate fills and cannot prove a next-minute fill. Automatic orders for this strategy therefore fail closed with `cross_market_next_minute_execution_unavailable`. Use `dry_run` for plans, `vnpy_paper` for gateway execution, and the minute execution model for historical replay. The built-in `DSA_SIM` may execute this strategy only with `DSA_SIM_MATCHING_MODE=next_minute_vwap`: it freezes a strict realtime quote at signal time, reads another provider-timestamped cumulative-volume and cumulative-turnover snapshot in the next minute, derives minute VWAP and volume from the deltas, and applies the same dynamic-slippage, limit-price, and 5% participation model. The two provider timestamps must be 45–90 seconds apart; a shorter span does not prove a complete next minute, while a longer span may cross multiple bars or a sleep interval, so both cancel fail closed. Tencent's provider-supplied daily limit-up and limit-down prices are passed directly to matching so special limits such as ST 5% are preserved; an A-share quote with neither provider limits nor a previous close is rejected. A snapshot older than 120 seconds, a non-advancing timestamp, regressing cumulative values, a provider change, or inconsistent volume/turnover deltas cancels the order instead of guessing a fill. Remainders after partial fills keep waiting and remain subject to the 60-second strategy revalidation. Cross-market orders also fail closed under every other `DSA_SIM` matching mode. The read-only status response exposes mode, delay, provider-span gate, and pending baseline count under `diagnostics.vnpy_bridge.matching`.

Order-status and trade callbacks for the same vn.py order are serialized within the process. Once a trade callback commits the Portfolio fill plus `fill_sync` and `execution` audit, a later order callback or MainEngine reconciliation may only observe the completed result; it cannot overwrite next-minute VWAP, slippage, fee, or fill-link evidence with an older submitted state. The campaign report continues to fail closed when any fill lacks execution evidence.

Without a bound account, `DSA_SIM` uses the paper setting's `initial_cash`. Once a campaign account is bound, each process start hydrates the gateway from that account's persisted Portfolio cash, position quantities, and average costs instead of silently returning to the gateway's CNY 1,000,000 default. After the event bridge attaches, the runtime clears stale position diagnostics and republishes the complete account snapshot. Individual position events merge by instrument and direction, while a zero-volume event removes the stale entry, so a post-restart exit cannot appear as a short position. Reconnects within the same process continue to preserve gateway state. Only declared simulator matching settings survive connect and reconnect; external broker credentials and unrelated fields are discarded before the local simulator is called.

## Risk And Acceptance

Every formal session that advances the 30-day target must contain fresh, continuously confirmed `NQ00Y` evidence under `nasdaq_futures_continuous_trend_available`. Missing, stale, or less-than-120-second trend evidence fails closed and may receive an intraday five-minute recheck; evidence collected after the formal run cannot retroactively qualify it.

The acceptance payload limits `qualified_execution_modes` and `qualified_trigger_sources` to the `vnpy_paper` / `vnpy_paper_auto` pair that can advance the 30-day target, and the run status must be `completed`. It discloses `dry_run` and zero-order sources that may form daily observations separately through `observation_execution_modes` and `observation_trigger_sources`; legacy `partial` run states fail closed and do not enter daily counting.

The optional point-in-time replay API accepts the same 12 tradeable V1.4 themes plus explicit opening/intraday phase, `entry_score`, `analysis_slot`, failed-breakout evidence, US close and premarket, `NQ00Y`, Asian supply-chain and market gates, board technicals, and next-day high-open exit evidence. It is diagnostic only and cannot advance the pure-forward 30-session campaign.

US premarket and close coverage normally requires a qualified provider timestamp for each theme basket. Because the core `mlcc` and `ccl` companies are primarily listed in Japan, Korea, and Taiwan, a missing qualified US ADR or mapped-basket quote may be supplemented only by a current, strong same-theme Asian supply-chain signal. Supplementation also requires the target US session's same-stage snapshot and an explicit component-coverage-insufficient record for that theme. A missing US session or calendar, an older schema without the theme record, a stage mismatch, a weak directional signal, or any other non-coverage failure cannot be hidden by Asian evidence. The runtime status exposes evidence completeness under `supplemented_themes`; active-theme diagnostics separately expose whether the Asian close or premarket supplement passed and which score source was used. No other theme can use this route, and missing, stale, or weak Asian evidence still fails closed.

Asian supply-chain reads revalidate every component provider timestamp. If a newer collection has insufficient coverage because of a transient provider failure, the service may select the latest complete snapshot that is still valid as live, lunch-break, or same-session official-close evidence. It exposes `latest_observed_at`, `skipped_newer_snapshot_count`, and `selection_reason`; the fallback expires immediately at its validity boundary and never carries into another trading day.

Same-session formal deduplication runs at the final `run_auto_trade_once` entry point, so UI triggers, API calls, scheduling, and the 15-second entry watcher share one guard. The first pass creates the formal baseline; later passes retry only candidates whose blockers are transient open/VWAP reclaim, source-board strength, technical evidence, or provider timestamp conditions and which have no active buy. High opens, extreme low opens, and account-risk blocks remain non-recoverable.

Every formal recovery must link to a baseline created within `[09:30, 09:35)` that day and persist both `formal_recovery=true` and `intraday_entry_recheck=true`. The 10:40 pass accepts only symbol-identified recoverable candidates; a recovery with a missing baseline, missing candidate identity, or a run outside `[09:30,09:35)` / `[10:40,10:42)` cannot submit a buy or advance the 30-day count. The 13:30/14:30 breadth runs are independent and never masquerade as formal recovery.

The default account starts at CNY 100,000, holds at most two stocks, directly targets 50% of current equity for each accepted symbol, and caps total exposure at 100%. An existing attributed position receives only the remaining gap to 50%. Pressure and gold evidence no longer reduce the position target, but they continue to affect scoring and hard gates. At most four daily buy orders are allowed. Fees, taxes, dynamic slippage, A-share lots, and T+1 remain enforced. Hard stop, take profit, and generic trailing drawdown remain 5%, 8%, and 4%. Generic maximum-holding and no-progress exits remain disabled in V1.4.

Expected gross edge must be at least the estimated full round-trip cost plus 0.5 percentage points. It first uses an explicit candidate forecast; a range entry uses the stock's distance to its Bollinger upper band; other entries use `75%` of the stock's ATR20 calculated from completed daily bars, capped by the 8% take-profit target. Board resistance distance, the current session high, and ranking score never substitute for stock expected return. Missing all auditable targets blocks the order.

Sell priority is fixed: 5% hard stop; severe external-market decline such as Korea or `NQ00Y`; eligible next-day stock high-open trailing exit; 8% take profit; range high/invalid range; then the generic 4% trailing drawdown. Later rules cannot overwrite an earlier reason, and every sell remains capped by T+1 sellability.

Use `GET /api/v1/vnpy-paper/cross-market-strategy/status` for frozen US premarket/close themes, Korea, Japan, Asian supply-chain, A-share open, CPO, and gold evidence. Use `GET /api/v1/vnpy-paper/cross-market-strategy/acceptance` for forward-campaign progress and `POST /api/v1/vnpy-paper/cross-market-strategy/acceptance/start` to start or reset it. `POST /api/v1/vnpy-paper/cross-market-strategy/migrate` explicitly moves V1.1/V1.2/V1.3 to V1.4 and resets the count; `target_account_id` is optional, and a same-account position is adopted only under the proven-ownership contract above. The target remains 30 distinct real A-share trading days with no historical-replay prerequisite.

Campaign start binds the current paper-account ID and opening equity. Later acceptance only counts Agent runs whose `settings.account_id` matches that bound account, so an account reset cannot mix two ledgers into one 30-day result. `GET /api/v1/vnpy-paper/cross-market-strategy/acceptance/report` returns an in-progress preview. Thirty fully evidenced sessions complete the forward window, but strategy acceptance additionally requires at least eight buy fills and five closed trades. A completed campaign below either threshold returns `final_insufficient_sample` with `strategy_accepted=false`; zero or sparse trading can never be presented as a passed strategy. The report includes net performance, drawdown, fees, taxes, the equity curve, and campaign transactions sourced from `cross_market_auto_entry` / `cross_market_auto_exit`. Every `DSA_SIM` fill carries its next-minute VWAP reference, model and realized slippage in bp, spread, minute volatility, participation, and directional slippage cost; aggregates expose explicit fees and taxes, slippage cost, all-in trading cost, and evidence coverage. `decision_audit` retains each formal `vnpy_paper` session's compact per-candidate source theme, action, status, blocker, score, data quality, risk flags, and trade plan. The primary equity curve, daily returns, and drawdown use persisted daily account snapshots, preserving zero-trade days and mark-to-market PnL, while the fill-level curve remains available as `execution_equity_curve`. A daily snapshot counts as closing evidence only when it was updated at or after `14:55` Asia/Shanghai on that A-share session; earlier snapshots are listed under `non_closing_dates`. The `cross_market_campaign_closing_snapshot` background task checks every five minutes and persists exactly one snapshot for the bound campaign account after 14:55 only when the current date is confirmed as an A-share trading day. It fails closed when the calendar or account ownership is uncertain, requires no Web-page visit, and never backfills a historical date. A fill without slippage evidence or an observation day without a closing snapshot produces an explicit warning. Its `report_window.date_to` is fixed to the thirtieth fully evidenced session and the account is replayed read-only as of that date, so later prices or transactions cannot move the result. Manual or other-strategy trades are reported as `account_contaminated` rather than silently included.

The Web `/paper-trading` page shows the bound account, 30-day progress, fully evidenced days, formal simulation days, buy/closed-trade sample thresholds, cumulative missing days for each evidence requirement, leading decision blockers, net PnL, all-in trading cost including slippage, and maximum drawdown. It distinguishes running, insufficient-sample, and accepted states and can export the campaign report with per-fill execution and per-candidate decision evidence as JSON.

Closing-equity evidence requires both an update at or after 14:55 on the corresponding A-share session and complete valuation quality. When positions exist, every position must use the strict quote route and persist its provider timestamp with the realtime price. A missing timestamp, one more than one second in the future, or one older than 120 seconds relative to the snapshot write fails closed; stale FX and valuation limitations also fail. A historical close, missing price, or limited valuation cannot substitute for the campaign close; the date enters `invalid_valuation_dates`, the background snapshot task keeps retrying, and final-report status remains blocked.

Acceptance status additionally exposes `formal_entry_time=09:30`, `formal_entry_window_seconds=300`, `legacy_formal_entry_time=09:35`, and `formal_execution_rejected_counts`. New runs use only `[09:30,09:35)`. A persisted legacy baseline marked with `analysis_slot=09:35` remains eligible under its original `[09:35,09:40)` contract so an in-progress campaign does not lose prior progress; this compatibility never schedules a new 09:35 baseline. An outside-window formal run is recorded as `formal_entry_outside_opening_window`; a recovery without a baseline is recorded as `formal_recovery_without_opening_baseline`. Both may remain observation evidence, but neither produces a valid `formal_execution` or a buy.

When several qualified observations exist for one A-share session, acceptance reporting selects a ready snapshot first, then the snapshot with fewer missing requirements, then a formal `vnpy_paper` run, and finally the newer snapshot on a tie. This only stabilizes daily reporting and never promotes degraded evidence into a tradable signal. If a later `dry_run` becomes the representative row, `session_results[].formal_execution` still preserves the date's formal run ID, state, evidence, missing requirements, and candidate/plan/submission/skip counts instead of overwriting the execution fact. The selected run is persisted in the response's `session_results`, so both the in-progress preview and final report can prove the 30-session gate day by day. These rows only reference real forward runs after campaign start; they never synthesize or backfill historical market data. The thirtieth ready date becomes `completion_session_date`; session and trade queries are bounded there. Risk exits and pending completion may continue through that session, while automatic trading and the intraday sell monitor return the healthy `paper_campaign_completed` skip on later Shanghai dates. If an order remains pending after the completion session, the next pass reconciles the gateway first: observed fills or terminal states converge from the real callback, while any still-active remainder is cancelled with `paper_campaign_completed`. Failed or skipped cross-market plans also cannot be resubmitted after campaign completion.

Historical JSONL replay remains available as an optional engineering diagnostic through `scripts/run_cross_market_historical_backtest.py`, but it neither starts nor blocks the forward campaign. The 30-day completion gate advances only for an A-share session whose formal `vnpy_paper_auto` run used `vnpy_paper` execution and had complete evidence at that time. This count is exposed as `qualified_paper_trading_days`, with `fully_evidenced_formal_vnpy_paper_run` as the completion basis. Timestamp-aligned `dry_run` and later zero-order observations remain useful evidence diagnostics, but they cannot advance the paper-trading target. After-hours drills, non-trading days, unqualified execution modes, and timestamp-misaligned observations also do not count. A ready A-share open, an audited US premarket universe, an audited US close-theme collection paired to the same US session, the same-session US first-hour/close pair, Japan market evidence, a continuous five-minute Korea gate, and the current gold signal make an observation fully evidenced. Incomplete coverage disables the affected technology theme without blocking per-stock scoring of an eligible domestic pharmaceutical fallback. `formal_execution_trading_days` and `fully_evidenced_formal_execution_days` separately prove whether the formal daily task ran and whether it had complete evidence at that moment; dates completed only by a later zero-order observation are listed separately and trigger a report warning without reducing the remaining-day count. CPO remains part of complete Asian evidence collection and technology-direction gating. All attempts remain visible through `observed_trading_days`, `fully_evidenced_trading_days`, `degraded_trading_days`, `data_completeness_pct`, and `missing_requirement_counts`.

Set `cross_market_observation_enabled=true` through `PUT /api/v1/vnpy-paper/settings` to run an independent zero-order observation without replacing the saved `auto_strategy`. The scheduler then collects Korea, US technology, CPO, and A-share open signals every 60 seconds, collects gold every five minutes, and runs `cross_market_paper_observation` every five minutes only during A-share sessions. Once their clock windows open, the A-share open and gold collectors first confirm an exchange-calendar session: weekends and exchange holidays skip without a quote request, while an unavailable calendar fails closed. This task always overrides execution to `dry_run`, must submit zero orders, and bypasses the order-capable failure-fuse latch so a recovered upstream can still produce a ready or degraded observation on the next pass. Formal `vnpy_paper` execution retains the fuse. When automatic paper trading is also enabled, the observation's first run is staggered two minutes after the 09:30 automatic trade. If an intraday restart aligns the five-minute observation with the 60-second sell monitor, the observation waits up to 60 seconds for the shared Agent lock and reports an explicit timeout only if contention persists. The formal daily task waits up to 120 seconds, so a same-second race cannot silently consume the day's order-capable run. It contributes at most one observed date after the campaign starts; readiness determines whether that date is classified as fully evidenced or degraded.

The zero-order observation keeps retrying every five minutes while the current session has no complete evidence. Once either a formal or observation run for the same account is fully evidenced, later rounds skip cheaply with `observation_already_fully_evidenced_today` instead of screening candidates again. This preserves recovery from missing evidence without adding unnecessary provider load or duplicate audit records.

`cross_market_intraday_entry_scan` checks every 15 seconds during `[09:30, 09:35)`, performs at most one bounded recovery during `[10:40,10:42)`, and runs one independent breadth-gated entry audit during each of `[13:30,13:32)` and `[14:30,14:32)`. Only the formal window creates the baseline; 10:40 unions same-account formal audits and retries symbol-identified recoverable candidates. The late runs rescreen, but stop before stock selection unless divergence, extreme decline, or repair is present. Current holdings, fills, active orders, cash, and account risk remain in duplicate-order and risk calculations.

After the formal task has run, a post-close restart schedules the next A-share session at 09:30. If sleep, shutdown, or a service outage misses `[09:30, 09:35)`, 10:40 does not create a late baseline or buy. The 13:30/14:30 slots may still record breadth and decide independently when a late-entry condition is present, but they do not replace the missed formal day.

The most recent run and the most recent formal `vnpy_paper_auto` run are persisted separately. `GET /api/v1/vnpy-paper/status` exposes `last_auto_run` and `last_formal_auto_run`; V1.4 readiness and system health use only the formal record, so a zero-order observation or independent intraday scan cannot appear as formal success. Restart scheduling accepts only a same-strategy, same-market, same-account, order-capable formal record for the current date.
Formal observations use schema 6 to separate full-universe collection audit from per-theme tradability. Daily acceptance requires the premarket record to identify the configured 91-symbol universe and all eleven mapped themes, contain at least one provider-time-validated component, and pair with a close collection for the same US session. The close record must enumerate all mapped themes and expose at least one usable theme. `available_theme_count`, `qualified_theme_count`, `supplemented_themes`, `missing_themes`, and full-coverage flags remain unchanged and still gate theme activation, candidate decisions, and intraday entries; missing coverage never becomes a trade signal. Only MLCC/CCL retain the qualified Asian supply-chain supplement. Schema 5 and older records keep their original full-coverage contract and are never retroactively promoted into the 30-session campaign.

On an intraday startup, calibration work that would contend with `[09:30, 09:35)` is moved beyond the entry window; the evidence monitor follows later. Order-capable paper execution takes priority over non-ordering calibration sampling.

The Windows long-running instance is hosted by `DailyStockAnalysis-CrossMarketPaper30D`.
